Reading [John Connell's The Running Book](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55625454-the-running-book) with its evocative 
portrayal of the Irish landscape prompted me to dig out this catalogue of 
black & white photographs by [Giles Norman](https://gilesnorman.com). 
   
I seem to remember we picked it up at his studio in Kinsale back in the early 1990's. Beautiful moody landscape and street photography.

We also had the best Seafood Chowder ever at a pub by the beach.
