If you have read Rebecca Solnit's articles at [Meditations in an Emergency](https://www.meditationsinanemergency.com) you know what to expect. The writing is sharp observed and deeply researched. The volume of information can make it a solid read at times but, I always came to the end of each chapter feeling I had learned something worthwhile. In several essays there's a focus on the relevance of the development of feminism and the fight for indigenous people's rights to the challenges we are faced with today.
